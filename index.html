Nestling Privacy

Last updated September 20, 2026

Nestling is a baby log that lives on your device. We do not create an account for you, we do not run a Nestling cloud, we do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we cannot see your feeds, diapers, sleep, medicine, or notes.

What stays on the device

Names, times, amounts, custom buttons, and alarm choices are stored in Nestling’s private storage on that phone, tablet, or computer. Closing the app keeps the log. Uninstalling Nestling, or clearing the site’s data in a browser, erases it on that device. That is how you delete your data — there is no Nestling account to close.

What you can send yourself

You may export a Nestling file and send it with AirDrop, Nearby, Files, USB, or another tool you choose. You may also pair two devices with a short code while you are together. The baby log travels encrypted from device to device. A public matchmaking network is used only to introduce those two devices (connection setup, not your log). We do not operate that network and we cannot see the transfer. If you send a file through Messages, email, or a drive, that service’s rules apply — not a Nestling server.

Children

Nestling is meant for parents and caregivers, not for children to use themselves. A child’s details are entered by you and stay under your control on your devices. We do not collect personal information from children, and we do not sell data.

Not medical advice

Nestling is a personal log, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace a clinician.

Permissions

Internet is used only when you start a short-code merge, so the two devices can find each other. Notifications and exact alarms are optional for the feed reminder. Nestling does not use your contacts, location, microphone, photos library, or advertising ID.
